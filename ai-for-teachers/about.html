About Kenny

More than twenty years of training and teaching, and I build AI systems too. This program is where the two meet.

Hello, I'm Kenny Rooney

For more than twenty years I have trained and taught adults, mostly in cultural awareness, soft skills and Business English. My work has focused on the UK, the US, Denmark, Germany, India, Thailand and China, and has included cultural awareness and soft skills coaching at Microsoft.

I also build AI systems: chatbots, lead-generation tools and content pipelines for businesses. Building them taught me where AI is reliable and where it isn't. That is most of what I teach in this program: how to get useful work out of it, and how to check what it gives you.

You know your subject, your students and your curriculum. I know how to teach people to use these tools well. Every teacher's materials are different, so I teach this one-to-one, on your own lessons, not from a slide deck.

Background at a glance

  • More than 20 years of training and teaching
  • Cultural awareness and soft skills coaching, including at Microsoft
  • DMIS-certified and trained in Cultural Detective
  • Business English and ESP teaching, including for IT professionals and corporate clients
  • Builds AI systems: chatbots, lead-generation tools and content pipelines

How I work

  • Plain language and no hype. I'll show you where AI saves time and where it doesn't.
  • Your materials, not generic examples. Every session starts from something you really teach.
  • Checking built in. You are the editor, and I teach you how to catch what AI gets wrong.
  • One-to-one, at your pace, with no slide deck to sit through.
